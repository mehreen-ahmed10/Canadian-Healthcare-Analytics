STOP UVICORN WITH CTRL+C.
Replace app/routers/dashboard.py with the version in this package.
Replace app/templates/dashboard.html.
Replace app/static/style.css and app/static/dashboard.js.
Replace app/main.py only if you need its Jinja template/static setup; this version keeps /api/health and includes dashboard router.
Keep app/database.py and .env unchanged.
Run: uvicorn app.main:app --reload
Open: http://127.0.0.1:8000
